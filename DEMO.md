# DubScript Studio — Demo Script (60–90 seconds)

**Format:** screen recording of `index.html` in a browser + voice-over (or captions).
**Goal:** problem → load → write → fit check saves a take → export.

---

### 0:00–0:12 — Hook / problem
**Say:** "If you dub donghua or anime into Hindi, you know this pain — you
translate a line, go to record it… and it just doesn't fit the scene."
**Show:** `index.html` open, empty sheet. Title visible:
*DubScript Studio by Prime Khatab Services.*

### 0:12–0:25 — Load subtitles
**Say:** "This is DubScript Studio. Give it any subtitle file…"
**Click:** **Load demo lines**.
**Show:** the table fills with 10 lines — timings, slot durations, original lines.
*(Alt: drag `sample.srt` onto the page instead, to show drag & drop.)*

### 0:25–0:45 — Write a dub line + fit check (the magic moment)
**Say:** "…and it builds your dubbing sheet. Now I write the Hindi line here…"
**Type (cue 1):** `Sect को चुनौती देने की हिम्मत कैसे हुई?`
**Show:** badge turns 🟢 **Fits**, with chars/sec count.
*(Verified: 39 chars in the 2.5s slot = 15.6 chars/sec → green.)*
**Say:** "Green means it fits the slot comfortably. But watch this…"

### 0:45–1:00 — Too-long warning
**Type (cue 2, deliberately long):**
`गुरुजी, उसकी साधना फिर से अगले स्तर पर पहुँच गई है, ये तो असंभव है, सब हैरान हैं!`
**Show:** badge turns 🔴 **Too long ✗ shorten it**.
**Say:** "Red — this line would never fit in 2.2 seconds. Better to know now
than after wasting a recording take. I shorten it…"
**Retype (cue 2):** `गुरुजी, वो फिर से मज़बूत हो गया है!`
**Show:** badge back to 🟢.

### 1:00–1:20 — Export
**Say:** "Sheet done? One click…"
**Click:** **Download dubbing sheet (.csv)** → open the CSV briefly in Excel/Sheets.
**Click:** **Download dubbed subtitles (.srt)** → show the file with Hindi lines.
**Say:** "CSV for my voice artists, and a ready SRT with the new lines for the video."

### 1:20–1:30 — Close
**Say:** "DubScript Studio — free, fully offline, no sign-up. Built by
Prime Khatab Services for dubbing creators everywhere."
**Show:** header/branding. End card text: **DubScript Studio — by Prime Khatab Services**.

---

**Recording tips**
- Zoom browser to 110–125% so the fit badges are readable.
- Keep mouse movements slow; pause 1 second on each badge colour change.
- Total spoken words ≈ 180–210 → lands at ~75–90 seconds at a natural pace.
